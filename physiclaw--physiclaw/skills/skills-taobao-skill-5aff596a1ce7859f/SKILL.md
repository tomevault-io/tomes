# 淘宝 (Taobao)

## 淘宝

- Append **非直播** to the query (e.g. `牛奶 非直播`) to filter out live-stream rooms.
- **Result cards tagged 直播中** are a shop's live room, not the item — skip them; tap a normal product card.

## 闪购

Tap the **闪购** tab at the top of the home page first, then search there (no **非直播** needed).

- **外卖:** pick the brand's nearest, fastest-delivering branch.
